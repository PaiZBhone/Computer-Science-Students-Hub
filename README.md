# CS_Student_Hub

The application includes the following main features:

~ Login Screen: Students must log in before accessing the application, providing a personalized environment for the Computer Science student community.
~ Home Tab: A social-media-style feed where students can view and interact with posts. Students can share department announcements, activities, events, funny moments, achievements, questions, and other content related to student life. Users can like and engage with posts.
~ Add Post Tab: Allows students to create and share their own posts. They can ask technical questions, share study materials and useful resources, talk about Computer Science topics, or simply post something fun for the community.
~ Notifications Tab: Keeps students updated with important activities such as assignment deadlines, upcoming exams, events, and interactions with their posts.
~ Student Community: Provides a space specifically for Computer Science students to socialize, make friends, share experiences, discuss their studies, and have fun together in a community similar to a small-scale social media platform.
